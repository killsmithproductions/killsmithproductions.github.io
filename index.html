INDEPENDENT · TEXAS · FOREVER FORGE FORWARD

Dyl the Killsmith is an independent Texas rap artist and creator building on his own terms.

Dyl the Killsmith brings you the underground records of modern-day Port Arthur, Texas. A Southeast Texas native, he represents the entire Golden Triangle, extending his reach to Houston and Texas culture as a whole. His gritty, eccentric, muddy style and country twang shape the country rap tunes heard throughout his evolution as an artist.

He began cultivating his vocals and music production skills after graduating from college in 2014. Convinced he could make better music than what he was hearing on the radio, he released Emergence in 2017. Available only on YouTube, its rough production captures an early stage of Dyl’s evolution as a producer.

Three more albums later, with a portfolio of over 60 records streamed more than 1,000,000 times worldwide, Dyl continues to evolve as an artist, singer, and songwriter—and, as of 2026, an author.

Privacy & signup details

Your first name and email are used to register your access to Road to the Stage. When signup is available, those details, the signup date, and your choice about release updates are sent to a private Google Sheet managed by Dyl the Killsmith. Only check the updates box if you want music news by email.

A first-party access cookie remembers your game access for 30 days. The embedded players connect to Spotify when loaded, including when you scroll to the crowd-favorite player. Playback starts when you press play; Spotify’s privacy policy applies. Opening the game, social links, Bandcamp, or Kindle takes you to those services.

Optional site measurement, when enabled, requires a separate choice. It uses button counts and page-performance measurements without sending your name, email, page URLs, or a visitor identifier. A local preference remembers that choice; any experiment assignment is removed when you turn measurement off.

For questions, removal of your signup details, or to stop release updates, email dylthekillsmith@gmail.com.
